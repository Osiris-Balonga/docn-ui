## Justification for added tests

Imposition receives one pure unit file because page transitions and cell coordinates are reusable geometry that PDF output alone would diagnose poorly. The label family extends one actual-PDF file for the three nominal compositions and the distinct partial-sheet risk. It compares final glyph positions with constants calculated independently from A4, 70 × 37 mm labels, 3 mm safe padding, and the requested margins. The existing catalog journey was extended rather than adding a new E2E file. No format × theme × cell matrix and no E2E per cell were added.

## Visual and document checks

Product renders at 70 × 37 mm with a strong name, reference, concise properties, and a printable QR. Address renders at 100 × 50 mm with recipient hierarchy, three address lines, and sender marker. Inventory renders at 100 × 50 mm with a prominent asset ID, location, date, accent marker, and QR. Poppler review found no clipping, overlap, blank page, distorted modules, or unreadable text.

The two-page A4 sheet intentionally begins at cell 19 of a 3 × 7 grid. Page one contains only recipients 1 and 2 at cells 19–20; page two contains recipients 3 and 4 at cells 0–1. Automated checks prove digital placement and ordering, not physical feed calibration. The printing guide requires 100% / Actual size, a plain-paper overlay trial, and margin adjustment for the real printer.

## Deviations and resumption

The lot specification mentioned public controls for dimensions, quantity, mode, and starting cell. The maintainer explicitly removed customization/export forms from template pages, so L10 preserves the accepted Charts-style preview/copy/source surface and exposes those capabilities through the installed source and typed render contract instead of restoring the rejected form.

The first sheet render exposed a legitimate zero-coordinate case: physical dimensions must be positive, while a cell origin may be zero. The sheet renderer now distinguishes positional conversion from dimension validation. Adding `core/imposition.ts` also increased the existing business-card consumer closure from 30 to 31 sources per project; `72c7f73` updates and strengthens that external assertion without reducing the closure.

No physical label stock or printer was available. Hardware margins, feed drift, adhesive behavior, cutting, and printed scale remain unverified. The dimensional presets are not Avery or other manufacturer certifications. PR #30 targets protected `dev`; the next permissible step is to observe exact-head required checks and request explicit merge authorization.
