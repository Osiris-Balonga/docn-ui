## Justification for added tests

Extend one business-card PDF family suite for actual dimensions, sides, content, and fixed-layout overflow. Add one focused form component suite for validation and renderer coordination, then one Playwright Chromium journey because browser worker preview and downloaded bytes cannot be proved by unit or component tests. Do not copy the journey for each composition or multiply theme/format combinations.

## Visual and document checks

The minimal French fixture produced a 10,142-byte, two-page PDF at exactly 240.945 × 155.906 pt per page. PDF.js extracted the identity, all four contact details, and the back-side brand while every measured text bound remained inside the 3 mm safe frame. The first 144 DPI rasterization exposed an e-mail/website collision between two columns; the layout was changed to aligned full-width rows, regenerated, and inspected with no remaining overlap or clipping. The back uses the controlled inverted theme color. PNG intermediates were removed; the PDF remains an ignored local artifact.

The built 1280 × 720 browser page was observed with the real first revision visible inside the PDF.js canvas, accessible editor labels, format/theme/language controls, page and zoom actions, and no global horizontal overflow. The passing Playwright journey then edited the name to `Anaïs Mavoungou`, displayed page 2, downloaded `docn-ui-business-card.pdf`, matched its byte length to the ready preview result, and independently extracted the edited name and exact page geometry. Contact-sheet and physical-print evidence remain pending.

S03 generated the editorial composition at 90 × 50 mm and the Studio composition at US 88.9 × 50.8 mm, then rasterized both sides at 144 DPI. A six-panel contact sheet combined those four pages with the minimal front/back. The review showed three different information structures, readable accents and contacts, no clipping, and a vector Studio QR with a four-module quiet zone. The raster and contact-sheet intermediates were removed after inspection. Automated final-PDF QR decoding and physical printing remain later qualification work.

The final G2 journey retained the last preview during an invalid empty name, disabled export, recovered after correction, reset the sample, applied 90 × 50 mm, and exported revision 5. The downloaded byte count matched the ready preview result, its input fingerprint differed from the initial revision, and independent PDF.js inspection found two 255.118 × 141.732 pt pages containing the final edited name. The obsolete `/qualification/pdf/` screen and its site component were removed; the low-level feasibility fixtures remain test evidence rather than a competing product path.
