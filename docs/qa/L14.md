## Visual and manual review

The ignored contact sheet at `.artifacts/l14/contact-sheet.png` contains all 21 pages from all 17 compositions. Review confirmed full-frame badge backgrounds and separate lanyard reverse, two complete business-card faces, four distinct invoice structures, three receipt proportions, multi-color report series/legends, and three distinct resume structures. Selected references live in `tests/visual/references`; their manifest records renderer versions, dimensions, hashes and a family-specific review rationale.

English template content and French accented text (`Bonjour Élodie`) survive actual PDF extraction in the PDF/consumer evidence. The production browser build was manually probed with clipboard permission: the copied Coral card command contained the exact current loopback registry item, and the opened reader exposed the versioned generated PDF download. Existing Chromium evidence covers keyboard page/family navigation, zoom, focus, code scrolling/copy controls and mobile layout. PDF tests inspect MediaBox/TrimBox/BleedBox geometry and receipt widths; this is digital geometry evidence only.

No physical printer, scanner, hardware barcode reader, actual Firefox, Safari, iOS Safari or screen-reader software was available. No card/sheet was printed at 100%; tickets and label sheets are outside current V1. The product therefore does not claim physical calibration, duplex registration, scanner grades, universal browser support, PDF/UA, PDF/X or CMYK.
